I am a fourth-year PhD student in the [Language Technology Lab](https://ltl.mmll.cam.ac.uk) at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk), supervised by [Anna Korhonen](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~alk23/) and [Edoardo Ponti](https://ducdauge.github.io/). During my PhD, I spent time at [Ai2](https://allenai.org/), working with Luca Soldaini and Valentin Hofmann, at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/) in Zürich, and at the [University of Edinburgh](https://www.ed.ac.uk/). Before that, I did my undergrad in AI at [JKU Linz](https://www.jku.at/en). I've been fortunate to gain research experience at [Cohere](https://cohere.com/), [Huawei Noah's Ark Lab](http://dev3.noahlab.com.hk) (2x) and [H2O.ai](https://h2o.ai/), as well as by [competing on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/bminixhofer).

My research aims to make language models cheaper to build and easier to change. Much of it is about [retrofitting](/retrofitting): reusing existing models instead of training new ones from scratch. Examples are moving or connecting a model to a new subword tokenizer ([Zero-Shot Tokenizer Transfer](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/532ce4fcf853023c4cf2ac38cbc5d002-Abstract-Conference.html), [Cross-Tokenizer Distillation](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/loc/san-diego/poster/119176)) or making subword-level LLMs operate over bytes instead ([Retrofitting Language Models to Operate over Bytes](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11111-4)). I also like building small, efficient task-specialized models, such as [Segment Any Text](https://github.com/segment-any-text/wtpsplit) for sentence segmentation.
